About

Four years in

I work on the ingestion and transformation layer — the part everything downstream depends on and nobody sees until it breaks. Most of that has been Airflow orchestration, dbt models, and platform-level warehouse design on Snowflake and Azure Databricks: first at Tata Consultancy Services on regulated financial services data, then at Saint Louis University.

The problem I keep coming back to is silent failure. An upstream schema changes, the pipeline runs green, and a report carries a wrong number for days. Nothing breaks — the data is just wrong. Most of what I've built since is some version of making that impossible: reconciliation checks, schema contracts, quality gates in CI. My M.S. is in Computer and Information Systems Security, which is where the instinct for governance and access control comes from.
